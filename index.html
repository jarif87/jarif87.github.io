About Me

I'm Sadik Al Jarif

Architect of Intelligent Systems | Defender of Digital Infrastructure | Bridging AI Innovation with Offensive Security

I am an AI/ML Engineer and Cybersecurity Practitioner with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering from Manarat International University (CGPA: 3.54/4.00). I specialize in developing production-grade artificial intelligence systems and conducting comprehensive offensive security assessments. As the Founder and CEO of NikaOrvion, I lead cross-functional initiatives delivering LLM-powered applications, computer vision solutions, and penetration testing services to clients across healthcare, education, and technology sectors.

My AI/ML expertise spans large language models, agentic systems, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and computer vision. I have practical experience fine-tuning transformers, building multimodal architectures, and deploying end-to-end pipelines using TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras, and Scikit-learn. I orchestrate agentic workflows with LangChain, LlamaIndex, LangGraph, and CrewAI, and manage the full MLOps lifecycle—including containerization with Docker, CI/CD automation, and cloud deployment on AWS, GCP, and Azure.

In cybersecurity, I perform penetration testing, vulnerability assessments, and web application security audits aligned with OWASP Top 10 standards. I am proficient with industry-standard toolchains including Metasploit, Burp Suite, Nmap, Nessus, Wireshark, and Scapy, and have developed custom intrusion detection systems and automated threat analysis frameworks. My offensive security experience covers network exploitation, malware analysis, wireless security testing, and incident response in controlled virtual environments.

I have shipped production tools including a Chrome extension with over 1,400 active users, and have delivered client projects in medical imaging, fraud detection, and supply chain optimization. I am committed to building robust, measurable solutions—whether reducing model inference overhead through ONNX quantization or identifying critical vulnerabilities through systematic red-team exercises. I am open to collaborations that advance secure, intelligent systems at the intersection of AI and cybersecurity.
